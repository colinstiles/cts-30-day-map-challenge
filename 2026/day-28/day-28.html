Lende Khola, from Langtang Lirung to Rasuwagadhi. A simulated sequence of 26 August 2026.

  1. Slope failure
  2. M5.2

Before 08:37 NPT

Rendered oblique view of an elevation model; not real footage and not a hazard map. Vertical scale is 1.15. The flood's path is an uncalibrated 2D diffusive-wave routing: 28 m of water on about 2 km² of the release, Manning n = 0.045. The drawn surface is 250 m above the channel, the middle of the 200–300 m wave observers reported at the border, not the model's own depth. The crossing is marked so the sequence can be read. Those shapes are not a survey. The failure, the M5.2 and later M4.2 signals, and the run of about 100 km follow the USGS Landslide Hazards Program preliminary page of 28 August 2026. The fall from about 5,200 m onto the glacier near 3,800 m, and the 08:37 and 08:45 times, follow Nepal’s NDRRMA Bhotekoshi–Trishuli update. Elevation is AWS Terrain tiles, mostly SRTM, with pits filled. Imagery is Esri World Imagery. USGS spells the river Lende; Nepali sources also use Lhende.
