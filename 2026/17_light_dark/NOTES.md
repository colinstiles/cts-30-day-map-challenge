# Day 17 — Light & dark

**Date:** November 17, 2026
**Working title:** Self-Transcendence 3100 Mile Race
**Tools:** JavaScript canvas, OpenStreetMap
**Page:** `2026/day-17.html`

## What is drawn

The line is the city block in Jamaica, Queens: 164th Place, the Grand Central Parkway frontage, 168th Street, and 84th Avenue (Abigail Adams Avenue). Thomas A. Edison Career and Technical Education High School and Joseph Austin Playground sit inside it. Centerlines are OpenStreetMap ways from a small extract of that block.

The certified lap is 0.5488 miles, 5,649 laps, 3,100 miles. Runners have 52 days, 30 August–20 October 2026, and the course is open from 6 a.m. to midnight. The animation counts those miles at an even daily pace. The figure on the block is the loop, not one counted lap.

The block is drawn, not tiled, so the streets stay sharp at this scale. Daylight behind the map is white. The block stays dark so the gold course and the street names keep their contrast. Each of the 52 days plays the open hours, night into daylight, at about five seconds a day, with no dusk hold. The course line starts thin and dim and gets brighter and heavier as the 5,649 laps accumulate. The ring is the 52 days. Gold is daylight inside the open window. Blue is the time before sunrise and after sunset. The mileage rides the leading edge of that ring. Sunrise and sunset are calculated for 40.716°N, 73.799°W, Eastern Daylight Time.
